Closer
 

A circle, not a quiz.

For 4–10 close people. Each session is a fresh climb: you start light and end somewhere honest. Everyone answers every question, going around the circle — the order reshuffles each card so no one is always first.

  • 1Read the card aloud. Then go around — everyone answers, in the order shown.
  • 2No advice, no fixing, no grilling. You listen, then it's the next person.
  • 3Don't want to answer? Tap your name to pass — then a dare instead. The group makes one up (or let the app pick if you're stuck). Do it, or take a strike toward the chicken crown.
  • 4The twist: the whole circle shares a pass budget — just 3. Passing keeps the Closeness meter climbing… until the budget runs out, and the next pass wipes the meter to zero. It refills smaller each time (3 → 2 → 1 → none), so the fuse keeps shrinking. Climb to Unbreakable together to win.
